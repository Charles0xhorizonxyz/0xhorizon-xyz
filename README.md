# 0xHorizon

**Digital infrastructure built for continuity.**

0xHorizon is an infrastructure project grounded in decentralised finance. We believe finance should offer equal and free access, sovereignty over one's own assets, and power held where it belongs: in everyone's hands. The technology we build is simply the means to get there.

## About

We work on foundational infrastructure: the protocols, systems, and technologies that enable trustless coordination at scale. Our work spans decentralised finance, digital asset systems, and the infrastructure required for durable, real-world decentralised ledger applications.

### Our Focus

- **Execution layers**: Rollups, modular chains, and scalable infrastructure
- **Interoperability**: Cross-chain bridges and liquidity routing
- **Real-world assets**: RWA integration and tokenization infrastructure
- **Wallets & abstraction**: Account abstraction and user experience layers
- **Identity & governance**: Decentralised identity and protocol governance tools

## Projects

Work in progress. Stables is the main contribution.

- **Stables** (in testing): a banking system merchants own. Self-custodial payments on the Minima blockchain, now in testing. [stablescouncil.org](https://stablescouncil.org/)
- **Minima Bay** (in development): real decentralised finance on Minima, with your assets in your own hands. [minimabay.org](https://minimabay.org/)
- **Mininotes** (live): a private paper pad for the people close to you. Notes stay sealed from device to device over Maxima. [mininotes.org](https://mininotes.org/)
- **Minima Venues** (beta): every MINIMA order book side by side, with a trade ticket and a paper market maker. Runs on your own computer. Free, open source. [github.com/Charles0xhorizonxyz/minima-venues](https://github.com/Charles0xhorizonxyz/minima-venues)
- **123go.taxi** (live): turns an address into a QR code a taxi driver scans to start the route in their own navigation app. Free, open source, nothing collected. [123go.taxi](https://123go.taxi/)
